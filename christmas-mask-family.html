Christmas Mask Family

CHARMING UNIQUE FESTIVE

Act Info

Christmas is here again!

Invite delightful festive cheer to your function or event with this charming and loveable Christmas Family.

Playful and unique, oversized and colourful - this is interactive mask theatre designed to spread all the Christmas joy!

This cheeky family is very keen to get all of their Christmas shopping done - and they may need your help.

With their bright soundscape of Christmas carols they create a festive and whimsical ambience that will connect directly to the hearts of your guests.

Some might call it mingling with a mischievous manner ...

A versatile act - trio format, roving and interactive.

Fabulous for Christmas Parties, Gala Events, Carols Events, Community Events, Corporate Christmas Parties and more.

Enquiries welcome.

Client Feedback

"The Mask Family were a terrific Covid-friendly program for 2020! In full face masks and gloves they came with their own PPE and their size and brightness keeps them safely physically distanced from audiences. In a general sense, the performers are masters of engaging general public in a joyous, silly and fun non-verbal experiences. The company members are consummate professionals, adapting, shifting and flexing to ensure the work lands perfectly in every context and their training, experience and ability is so evident in their ensemble work and their subtle shifts." Brisbane Festival, QLD
